Get PsoV3.dol

Building the pso-ios app needs one file from your copy of Phantasy Star Online Episode I & II Plus (GameCube, USA): the game program, PsoV3.dol. This page copies it out of your disc image.

Your disc image stays on your device. This page runs entirely in your browser: it reads the few megabytes it needs from the file and uploads nothing.

Works on a computer or in Safari on an iPhone or iPad. It reads RVZ files compressed with Zstandard, Dolphin's default; for other RVZ settings, convert the image to ISO or RVZ (Zstandard) in Dolphin.
